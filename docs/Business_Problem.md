# Business Problem

Clinical Trial organizations collect large volumes of operational data from hospitals, research sites, and patient enrollment systems.

These operational systems are optimized for daily transactions rather than analytical reporting.

Typical business questions include:

- Which site recruited the highest number of patients?
- Which study is progressing faster?
- Which therapeutic area has the highest enrollment?
- What is the monthly enrollment trend?

Running these reports directly against OLTP databases is slow and inefficient.

Therefore, organizations build a Data Warehouse optimized for analytics.
